# Spec — 8-bit mode: everything in pixel art, and juice

## Problem

The 8-bit skin, the Easter egg of the site, is a partial reskin and a silent one.

- **The pixel font stops at headings, labels and buttons.** Paragraphs, cards, the footer and the
  Inspector keep the body font.
- **The icons are vector drawings.** Ten icons of `lucide-react`, the three social logos, the arrow of
  the back-to-top button and the `→` of the main call to action keep their smooth curves. Only the
  magnifier of the Inspector and the mini sprite are pixel art.
- **The technology logos** of the band are the same nine SVG files in both skins.
- **Nothing reacts.** There is no sound, a click moves a button by four pixels and nothing else, the
  only thing that moves on its own is the band of technologies, and the skin changes with a cross-fade.
- **The stacked scroll** — a panel pins and the next one slides over it — stays active inside the skin,
  where the page is meant to read as the stages of a game.

## Expected outcome

Inside the 8-bit skin everything on screen is pixel art and the page answers like a game — sound,
particles, a living background, a HUD, achievements — and a visitor who never enters the skin pays for
none of it.

## Scope

1. **Normal scroll inside the skin.** The panels do not pin and do not overlap; each one is a screen of
   its own in normal flow.
2. **Every text in the pixel font**, body copy included.
3. **Pixel-art icons** for every icon of the interface, drawn on a 16×16 grid.
4. **Pixel-art technology logos**, generated by `npm run assets:pixel`.
5. **Shape:** notched pixel corners, a square scrollbar, scanlines over the portrait and the
   thumbnails, a blinking selector on the links of the navbar, the tagline of the hero as a dialogue
   box, a pixel cursor.
6. **Sound effects**, synthesised in the browser, on by default inside the skin, with a mute control
   whose state persists.
7. **Controls:** the mute button in the navbar, and a PAUSE button that opens a menu with sound, music
   and effects switches, the level and the list of achievements.
8. **Animation on interaction:** pixel particles on a click, a short shake when the skin is entered and
   when a carousel hits its end, a wipe in bands when the skin changes, scroll reveals in steps.
9. **Animation on screen:** an idle sprite that jumps when clicked, pixel clouds (light theme) or
   twinkling stars (dark theme), and geometric shapes drifting behind the content, with parallax.
10. **HUD:** an XP bar that fills with the scroll, a notice of the stage when the section changes, and a
    level equal to the years of experience.
11. **Achievements:** eight, announced by a toast and kept in the browser.
12. **Music:** an original chiptune loop, off whenever the page loads.
13. **RPG dialogue:** the tagline of the hero is typed letter by letter.

## Out of scope

- **Juice or sound in the normal skin.** The requester chose the 8-bit skin as the only place for it.
- **Audio files.** Every sound is synthesised, so there is nothing to license or to download.
- **Keyboard shortcuts for the mute.** A single-key shortcut needs a way to turn it off or remap it
  (WCAG 2.1.4); the buttons are enough.
- **A blinking frame for the sprite.** It needs a second drawing of the portrait.
- **Gamepad input.**
- **New tabs or measurements in the Inspector.** It inherits the font and the close icon, nothing else.
- **`site.contentUpdatedAt`.** The copy a crawler reads does not change.

## Acceptance criteria

The gate, green in the five Playwright projects:
`npm run lint:eslint:check && npm run lint:prettier:check && npm run build && npm run test:e2e`

- [x] **The normal skin is untouched.** No request for `/tech/8bit/`, `/pixel/`, `/avatar/avatar.png`
      or `/thumbnails/8bit/`; no `AudioContext` is created after the page is scrolled through and
      clicked; the pixel font stays `unloaded`. Screenshots of `/` and `/en` at 1440×900, 1024×768 and
      390×844, in both themes, are identical to those of the build before the work.
- [x] **Budget**, against a measurement taken before the first change and recorded in `findings.md`:
      the script of the first load grows by at most 1.5 KB (`npm run audit`), the document by at most
      4 KB and the stylesheet by at most 2 KB, both gzipped. `npm run audit` and
      `npm run audit -- --path=/en` report at least 95 in every category.
- [x] **Scroll:** in the 8-bit skin, for every pair of sections, the end of a panel is where the next
      one starts, within 1 px. In the normal skin the tests of the stack pass unchanged.
- [x] **Text:** in the skin, `body`, a paragraph, a card and the Inspector compute to Pixelify Sans; no
      text is smaller than 12 px; the page does not scroll sideways at 320 px, in `pt` and `en`.
- [x] **Icons and logos:** in the skin every `[data-icon]` displays its pixel drawing and hides the
      vector one; the logos come from `/tech/8bit/` with `image-rendering: pixelated`. Running
      `npm run assets:pixel` again leaves `git status` clean. The requester approves the contact sheet
      of the icons, the logos and the cursors.
- [x] **Sound:** with `AudioContext` spied on, entering the skin and clicking a button starts
      oscillators; muted, none starts; after a reload the mute holds (`aria-pressed` and
      `localStorage`).
- [x] **Effects:** on, and with motion allowed, the scenery has running animations and a click creates
      a burst of particles. Off, or under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the runtime runs no
      animation, creates no burst, and the tagline is shown whole at once.
- [x] **HUD:** the XP bar is empty at the top of the page and full at its end; scrolling to `#projects`
      shows the label of that section in the stage notice.
- [x] **Achievements:** the first entry shows the toast of the Easter-egg achievement in a
      `role="status"` region; it persists and the PAUSE menu lists "1/8"; entering with the Konami code
      unlocks a second one.
- [x] **Music:** off when the page loads; turning it on keeps scheduling notes; a reload turns it off.
- [x] **Dialogue:** while and after the tagline is typed, its accessible text is `hero.tagline`.
- [x] **Accessibility:** no axe violation in either theme with the skin on, with the PAUSE menu open as
      well; every new button is a 44 px target below `lg`; the toasts do not intercept clicks.
- [x] **Languages:** nothing of the PAUSE menu, the HUD or the toasts is left in the source language at
      `/en`.
- [x] **Manual check** on `npm run dev`: `/` and `/en`, light and dark, a wide window and the width of a
      phone, the skin on and off; the sound checked by ear in Chrome and in Safari.
